# 📖 Devotee & Administrator User Guide

Welcome to the **Sri Rama Seva Committee Digital Platform User Guide**. This manual guides devotees, donors, and temple committee administrators through the core portal features.

---

## 🚩 1. Devotee Public Website & Mobile App

### E-Hundi Donation & 80G Receipts:
1. Navigate to **E-Hundi** section on the website or mobile app.
2. Select your desired donation amount (e.g. ₹501, ₹1,008, ₹5,000, or custom).
3. Check **Require 80G Tax Exemption Certificate** and enter your **PAN Card Number**.
4. Click **Proceed to Pay** to complete payment via UPI, Credit/Debit Card, or Netbanking.
5. Once payment is confirmed, your official **80G Tax Exemption PDF Receipt** will be available for instant download.

### Booking Puja Seva:
1. Navigate to **Seva Booking** tab.
2. Choose your preferred Puja (Nithya Archana, Sri Sita Rama Kalyanam, Annadhanam).
3. Enter your **Gotram** and **Nakshatram** for divine Sankalpam chantings.
4. Select your preferred date and enable **Prasad Speed Post Delivery** to receive holy akshintalu & prasad at home.

---

## 💼 2. Temple Administration ERP Portal

### Financial Ledger & 80G Receipt Issuance:
1. Login to Admin Portal at `/donations`.
2. View real-time donation ledger entries with status (`CAPTURED`, `PENDING`).
3. Click **Generate 80G PDF** to issue official receipts manually if requested by offline donors.
4. Use **Send WhatsApp / Email** to dispatch digital receipts to devotees.

### Razorpay Payment Reconciliation:
1. Navigate to `/reconciliation`.
2. Review automated settlement matching between Razorpay payment IDs and bank credit notifications.
3. Identify flagged discrepancies instantly with 100% audit logging.
